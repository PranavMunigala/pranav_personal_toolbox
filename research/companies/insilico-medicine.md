# Insilico Medicine

## Company Overview
- **HQ**: Hong Kong, with R&D/management hubs in the US, mainland China, Taiwan, UK, Belgium, and Abu Dhabi.
- **Founded**: 2014, by **Dr. Alex Zhavoronkov** (Chairman/CEO) and **Dr. Alex Aliper** (President).
- **Thesis**: apply deep learning to biology/chemistry to compress the slow, expensive drug discovery process (~10-15 years, $1-2B+ per approved drug).
- **Stage**: IPOd on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange in Dec 2025 (~HKD 2.28B raised), the first AI-driven biotech to list on the HKEX Main Board — now a public hybrid tech/biotech company, not a pure R&D startup.

## Technology & Products
End-to-end platform, **Pharma.AI**, spanning the full R&D pipeline — the differentiator vs. narrower point-solution competitors is that these three pieces feed into each other rather than being sold/used separately:

- **PandaOmics** (target identification) — mines multi-omics data, literature, patents, and trial data to rank disease targets by evidence strength. Think of it like a search/ranking algorithm: instead of ranking web pages by relevance, it scores thousands of candidate disease targets and returns a shortlist with the evidence behind each score.
- **Chemistry42** (molecule generation, launched 2020) — 42 generative algorithms (deep generative models, RL, transformers) that design and optimize drug-like molecules and predict ADMET/toxicity properties. This is generative modeling applied to chemistry: instead of hand-designing a molecule atom-by-atom, the model samples candidate structures from a learned distribution over drug-like molecules, the same underlying idea as an image-generation model sampling pixels. Licensed to 20+ pharma companies (15+ external / 30+ internal programs), including published work with Janssen on neuroscience targets.
- **inClinico** (trial outcome prediction) — forecasts a clinical trials probability of success from historical data and trial design. Functionally a classifier trained on the outcomes of past trials, applied to a new trials features — like a weather forecast for a trial before its run.

**Proof point — rentosertib** (formerly INS018_055): a TNIK inhibitor for idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF), notable because its both **AI-discovered** (target found by PandaOmics) and **AI-designed** (molecule generated by Chemistry42) — one of the first AI-designed drugs to show a real human efficacy signal:
- **Phase IIa** (2024, n=71): +98.4 mL lung-function improvement at 60mg vs. -20.3 mL for placebo.
- **Phase III**: initiated July 2026 — a 320-patient, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study across 47 centers in China, once-daily dosing over 52 weeks.[^2026-08-04]
- Insilico reports **40+ pipeline programs** (24 disclosed), **13 with IND clearance** as of early 2026.
- Full pipeline: https://insilico.com/

## Market & Competition
- **Revenue streams**: (1) Pharma.AI software licensing — 13 of the top 20 global pharma companies reported as licensees/partners — and (2) its own pipeline, notably a **March 2026 collaboration with Eli Lilly** worth up to **$2.75B** ($115M upfront, rest milestone/royalty-based).
- **Competitors**: Schrodinger, Recursion Pharmaceuticals (absorbed Exscientia), other AI-target/AI-chemistry biotechs, and pharma building in-house AI.
- **Edge**: breadth (target ID + molecule design + trial prediction under one platform) plus real clinical proof points most competitors lack.
- **Why the market is hard**: AI reliably shortens the discovery/design phase, but doesnt yet reliably raise clinical trial success rates — molecules still have to survive real trials in real patients.

## Careers & Personal Fit
- No formal internship/co-op program on the public careers page (insilico.com/careers); open roles skew senior (e.g. Machine Learning Engineer in Abu Dhabi, Senior Machine Learning Scientist, Computational Protein Design Scientist).
- One Business Development Manager posting explicitly welcomes exceptional recent graduates.
- Historically sources talent via **hackathons/competitions** in the US, Greater China, Canada, and the Middle East rather than campus recruiting — likely a more realistic entry point than a posted internship.
- General applications: career@insilico.com.
- **Fit**: good for someone wanting generative AI/ML work that plugs into a real clinical pipeline, in a fast-moving, publicly-traded-but-still-lean organization.

## General Notes & Personal Takeaways
- Rentosertib is a genuinely rare concrete proof point — most AI drug discovery companies lack a comparable clinical readout, and its now advancing through Phase III rather than stalling after early trials.
- The Lilly deal and HKEX IPO suggest the market is starting to believe the platform.
- **Drawbacks**: the Glassdoor culture signal is a real yellow flag, and there is no formal early-career pipeline.
- **Watch**: the Phase III rentosertib readout (320-patient study, 52-week endpoint), how Lilly milestone payments materialize, and whether post-IPO public-market pressure changes R&D pace/culture.
- **Personally**, the **Chemistry42** generative-chemistry approach was the most interesting part of the platform — sampling candidate drug molecules from a learned distribution, the same underlying idea as generative image models, applied to real chemistry.[^2026-08-04-b]
